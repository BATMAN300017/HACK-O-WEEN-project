Over 90% of conventional fraud alerts are false positives, burning out analysts and allowing sophisticated money-mule rings and account-takeover attacks to slip through disconnected spreadsheets and black-box classifiers.
The Solution: SentinelFlow AI
SentinelFlow AI is the first Self-Directed Fraud Investigation Command Center that bridges the gap between raw signal detection and final authorized disposition.
Why SentinelFlow AI Wins
Investigation System, Not Merely a Classifier: SentinelFlow does not output an opaque score and stop. It automatically launches a 13-step self-directed evidence-gathering sequence that retrieves multi-hop counterparties, compares account-specific MAD baselines, identifies shared devices, and produces a court-ready case dossier with SHA-256 integrity hashes.
Multi-Signal Hybrid Risk Ensemble: Combines 4 distinct modalities: Explainable Rules (30%), Account Rolling MAD Anomalies (25%), Supervised Scikit-Learn Logistic Regression (25%), and Network Graph Topology Signals (20%).
Governed Continuous Learning with Strict Label Policy: Never poisons models with unverified alerts. Only human-investigated confirmed fraud and verified false positives enter the versioned training dataset. Promotion requires meeting strict holdout precision (≥0.70) and sample thresholds.
Zero External Paid Dependencies: Runs 100% locally with deterministic reproducibility, complete automated test coverage, and an integrated interactive command center UI.
