Platform Label Policy (Responsible AI Safeguard)

To prevent model degradation and feedback loops, SentinelFlow AI enforces a strict label policy:

Confirmed Fraud → Positive Label (1)
Requires authorized analyst disposition and peer review where mandated.
False Positive → Negative Label (0)
Legitimate activity verified by analyst. Contributes directly to reducing false positives.
Inconclusive / Suspicious → Excluded
Excluded from binary classification training sets to prevent noise contamination.
